Smart Farming Tips

Optimal Soil Moisture

Paddy farming requires 60–80% soil moisture during the vegetative phase. Monitor regularly to prevent water stress and ensure nutrient uptake.

Balanced Fertilizers

Using the right NPK ratios is critical. Over-fertilization damages soil health and reduces long-term yield.
