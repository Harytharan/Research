About PaddyPredict

What This System Does

PaddyPredict brings together multiple AI models to help paddy farmers make data-driven decisions at every stage of the farming cycle.

Estimate Farming Costs

Calculate seed, fertilizer, pesticide, labor, water, and machinery costs with AI-powered prediction to plan your budget effectively.

Recommend Fertilizers

Get top-3 AI-recommended fertilizers optimized for your soil conditions, growth stage, and desired yield target.

Detect Pests & Diseases

Upload leaf images or use sensor data to identify paddy pests and diseases instantly with CNN-based deep learning models.

Predict Prices & Demand

Forecast rice market prices and demand trends using LSTM and XGBoost models to time your sales perfectly.
